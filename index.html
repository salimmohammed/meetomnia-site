Why fixed price instead of hourly?

Because hourly billing rewards slow work. A fixed price puts my incentive where yours is: the outcome. You know exactly what you get and what it costs before we start.

A few hours of stakeholder time for interviews and the readout. I do the analysis, synthesis, and writing — your team keeps shipping.

If you’re about to spend real money building, yes — a $7,500 scoping sprint is cheap insurance against a $100,000 wrong turn.

You’ll hear it on the free call, not after an invoice. If a sprint won’t help, I’ll say so and point you at what will.
