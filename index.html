Featured Projects

Fintech

Challenge
Monitor critical information across news articles, research reports, quantitative models, events, earnings and more for financial professionals.
Solution
Developed an AI-powered “Watchlist Pulse” app, utilizing NLP (natural language processing) to efficiently scan, summarize, and quantify data, which provided actionable insights and alerts on portfolios.
Highlight Metrics
This innovation not only won the Best Technical Product Award but also significantly improved client engagement and decision-making capabilities.

Legaltech

Challenge
Highly valued, customer-requested eSignature feature was consistently back-burned.
Solution
Built an integrated eSignature functionality, natively into an existing platform using PLG techniques including in-app activation and onboarding.
Highlight Metrics
Increased APRU (average revenue per user) by 10% and resulted in adoption by 25% of the client base in the first 4 weeks.

InsureTech

Challenge
The property insurance sector required a more precise and less intrusive method for property underwriting.
Solution
Pioneered an AI solution, using ML (machine learning) to automatically extract property attributes from aerial imagery to conduct fully virtual, remote property inspections.
Highlight Metrics
Decreased assessment times from 6 weeks to 5 minutes and allowed carriers to bind policies at time of quote. Priced at a premium (380% of current solutions).

Data Analytics and Business Intelligence

Challenge
Investment banks lacked crucial insights into private equity holdings, transactions, and associated fees.
Solution
Created a ground breaking analytics solution, providing banks with detailed dashboards, customization capabilities, and analytics enabling strategic decision-making and resource allocation decisions.
Highlight Metrics
Reduced turnaround time from 90 days to overnight and Increased transparency across hundreds of thousands of transactions.
